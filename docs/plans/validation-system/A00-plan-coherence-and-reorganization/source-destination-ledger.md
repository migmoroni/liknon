## Disposition Vocabulary

- `Rewritten`: active substance now belongs to the listed target.
- `Split`: substance was separated among multiple conceptual owners.
- `Superseded`: a more precise active owner already covers the requirement.
- `Archived`: retained unchanged only as historical evidence.

Every source listed below is archived after its active substance has a target.
Active plans do not depend on archived text.
