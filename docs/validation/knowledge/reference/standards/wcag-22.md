# WCAG 2.2

## Authority Scope And Version

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 is a W3C Recommendation dated
5 October 2023. It defines testable success criteria for Web content under the
principles perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.
[Source: w3c.wcag-2.2#status-and-scope](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#abstract)

The exact 2.2 version is intrinsic to this standard's identity rather than a
tool patch used only for provenance.

## Native Terminology And Conformance

WCAG retains Levels A, AA, and AAA. Conformance at a level requires satisfying
all criteria at that level and lower levels together with the conformance
requirements. W3C does not recommend AAA as a general policy for entire sites
because some content cannot satisfy every AAA criterion.
[Source: w3c.wcag-2.2#conformance-levels](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#levels-of-conformance)

## Applicability

Apply WCAG conformance language to Web content within an explicitly identified
scope, technologies relied upon, pages or processes, and level. Terminal or
native interfaces may use relevant principles only with a clear statement that
WCAG conformance is not being claimed outside its defined scope.

## Evidence And Limitations

Automated tools can observe selected machine-detectable failures. Code review,
keyboard and visual inspection, and assistive-technology evaluation remain
necessary where meaning, interaction, or user experience determines a success
criterion. Passing one tool or sample is not proof of complete conformance.

Evaluation may require browsers, assistive technology, representative content,
and people with appropriate expertise. Record cost, environment, user data,
external services, and any mutations or publication involved.

## Misuse To Avoid

Do not translate A, AA, and AAA into a universal security, maturity, quality, or
risk scale. Do not claim complete conformance from automation, omit the scope of
a claim, or silently substitute another WCAG edition.

## Sources And Next Step

Use the linked W3C Recommendation as the normative source. Identify affected
users and exact success criteria, then use [Validation Strategy](../../explanations/foundations/validation-strategy.md)
to compose proportional automated and human evidence while preserving WCAG's
native terminology.
